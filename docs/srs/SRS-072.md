# Multiplier signs

`f_mul[i]` **shall** be `>= 0` when row `i` is at its lower bound and `<= 0` when at
its upper bound, `x_mul` **shall** follow the same convention for bounds on `x`, and
`f_mul[obj_row]` **shall** be 0.
