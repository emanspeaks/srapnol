# Document conventions

Every normative item has a unique identifier of the form `SRS-nnn`, a
`verification-method`, a `category` (functional, interface, performance, safety,
security, quality, constraint, environment, packaging), a `status` (`pending`: not
started; `surface`: types and signatures exist, behavior does not; `done`: implemented
and its procedures pass), and, where the reason is not obvious, a `rationale`.
Section headings and informative notes are non-normative items with word identifiers
(`SRS-PRB`, `SRS-PURPOSE`); they are never cited as requirements.
