# Optimality termination

The solver **shall** exit `optimal` when both hold: the largest nonlinear row
violation scaled by `1 + ||x||` is at most `Options.major_feasibility_tolerance`, and
the largest scaled complementarity or reduced gradient scaled by `max(1, ||pi||)` is at
most `Options.major_optimality_tolerance`, with the norms defined in DES following the
SNOPT manual.
