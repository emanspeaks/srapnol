# Finite-difference steps

Forward-difference steps **shall** be `Options.difference_interval`\((1 + |x_j|)\),
central-difference steps **shall** be
`Options.central_difference_interval`\((1 + |x_j|)\), and `Options.function_precision`
**shall** decide when to switch from forward to central differences.
