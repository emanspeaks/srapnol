# QP subproblem

At major iterate \((x_k, s_k, \pi_k)\) the solver **shall** minimize
\(g_k'd + \tfrac{1}{2}d'H_k d\) over \(d = x - x_k\) subject to
\(F_k + J_k d - s = 0\) and the bounds on \(x\) and \(s\), by minor iterations that
maintain a basic/superbasic/nonbasic partition and a dense Cholesky factor \(R\) of
the reduced Hessian \(Z'HZ\).
