# Merit function

The solver **shall** accept steps by a line search on the augmented Lagrangian
`M(x, s, pi) = f - pi'(F - s) + 1/2 (F - s)' D (F - s)` over the nonlinear rows, with
the diagonal penalty `D` updated by the rule of Gill, Murray, and Saunders (2005).
