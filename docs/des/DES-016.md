# QP subproblem and minor iteration

Formulation: minimize `g_k'(x - x_k) + 1/2 (x - x_k)' H_k (x - x_k)` subject to
`F_k + J_k (x - x_k) - s = 0` and the bounds on `x` and `s`.

Minor iteration:

1. Multipliers: solve `B' pi = g_B`; reduced costs `sigma_N = g_N - N' pi`.
2. Optimality of the current face: `Z'g` small and every nonbasic reduced cost of the
   right sign within tolerance ends the QP.
3. Pricing: otherwise, if `Z'g` is small, the nonbasic with the worst-signed reduced
   cost becomes superbasic; `nS += 1` and `R` gains a column.
4. Direction: solve `R'R p_S = -Z'g`; `p = Z p_S`.
5. Ratio test: the largest `alpha <= 1` keeping every basic and superbasic within
   bounds, with EXPAND (Gill et al. 1989) to guarantee nonzero steps and prevent
   cycling under degeneracy.
6. Update: if a basic hit a bound, swap it with a superbasic (LU update, rank-one
   correction of `R`); if a superbasic hit a bound it becomes nonbasic and its column
   leaves `R`; `alpha = 1` means the face's minimizer was reached.

Before the first `f` evaluation a feasibility-phase QP with no objective finds a
linearly feasible `x_0`, exiting 11 or 12 if none exists; every later QP keeps the
linear rows feasible, so `f` is evaluated only at linearly feasible points.
