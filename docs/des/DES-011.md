# Scaling

With `scale_option != .none`, row and column factors `r_i`, `c_j` come from the
iterative geometric-mean procedure of Fourer (1982) on `[A ; G(x0)]`, so scaled entries
`r_i J_ij c_j` have magnitudes near 1; the solver works on `x' = x / c`, `F' = r F`.
Everything user-visible is unscaled on the way in and out, callbacks included. The
truss problem is the motivating case: `x ~ 1e-4`, `F ~ 1e8`, `G ~ 1e12`.
