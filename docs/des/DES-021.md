# SQP driver and termination

Major iteration `k` holds `x_k`, `s_k`, `pi_k`, the penalty vector `D_k`, and `H_k`:

1. Evaluate `f(x_k)`, `G(x_k)`; form `F_k`, `J_k`.
2. Solve the QP for `(d_x, d_s)` and `pi_hat`.
3. Update `D` for descent along `(d_x, d_s, pi_hat - pi_k)`.
4. Line search for `alpha`; update `x`, `s`, `pi`.
5. Update `H`.
6. Convergence test; otherwise repeat.

Convergence (SNOPT manual): primal `max_i viol_i / (1 + ||x||_inf) <=
major_feasibility_tolerance` over nonlinear rows (linear rows and bounds are within
`minor_feasibility_tolerance` by construction); dual `max_j comp_j / max(1, ||pi||) <=
major_optimality_tolerance` with `comp_j` the complementarity of variable or slack `j`
(`sigma_j min(x_j - l_j, 1)` at a lower bound, `-sigma_j min(u_j - x_j, 1)` at an upper
bound, `|sigma_j|` when free) and `||pi||` a scaled 2-norm. `Result` reports both
quantities. Limits exit 31, 32, 33; violation beyond `violation_limit` exits 22.
Exits fill `State` with the unscaled final iterate, full `F`, states, and multipliers
with the snOptA sign convention.
