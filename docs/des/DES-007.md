# Data flow

`Problem` and `Options` enter `Solver.init`, which validates, converts `A` and `G` to
CSC, computes scale factors, and sizes the workspace from `n`, `nf`, `nnz(A)`, `nnz(G)`,
and the options. `solve` reads `State` (start point and, for `basis`/`warm`, states and
multipliers), runs the major loop entirely inside the workspace, and writes the final
iterate, full `F`, states, and multipliers back to `State`, returning `Result`. The
callback is the only outward call in the loop; the writer, when present, receives log
lines at iteration boundaries.
