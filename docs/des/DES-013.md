# Dense SIMD kernels

Dot, axpy, scale, 2-norm, infinity-norm, elementwise min/max/abs, bound projection,
and infeasibility sums are written over `@Vector(w, f64)` with
`w = std.simd.suggestVectorLength(f64)` (scalar when null) and a scalar tail. Each
kernel has a scalar twin used by the tests and as the fallback.
