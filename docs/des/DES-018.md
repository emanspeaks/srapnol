# Merit function and line search

Merit: `M(x, s, pi) = f(x) - pi'(F(x) - s) + 1/2 (F(x) - s)' D (F(x) - s)` over the
nonlinear rows, `D = diag(rho_i)`, `rho_i >= 0`. The search direction is the combined
`(d_x, d_s, d_pi)` with `d_pi = pi_hat - pi_k`. GMS show the QP step is a descent
direction once `D` is large enough; the update raises only the `rho_i` needed to make
`M'(0) <= -1/2 d' H d` and lets them decay slowly afterward (Gill et al. 1992).

Step: a safeguarded polynomial search (quadratic, then cubic) on
`phi(alpha) = M(x + alpha d_x, ...)` seeks `phi(alpha) <= phi(0) + mu alpha phi'(0)`
with `mu = 1e-4` and `|phi'(alpha)| <= eta |phi'(0)|`, `eta = linesearch_tolerance`.
The first trial step is `min(1, major_step_limit (1 + ||x||) / ||d_x||)`. On
`error.Undefined` the step is halved and retried until a floor triggers exit 63; when
no step gives sufficient decrease after a Hessian reset, exit 41. Objective decrease
below `-unbounded_objective` or a step beyond `unbounded_step_size` exits 21.
