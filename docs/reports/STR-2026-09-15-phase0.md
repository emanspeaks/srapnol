## 1. Overview of results

- **Conclusions.** Every automated procedure that exists passes; every solve procedure
  skips, as the acceptance rule permits while `Solver.solve` is a stub and its
  requirements are `pending`. The test rig, reference problems, and Jacobian oracle
  are validated and ready for phase 1. The documentation tree validates with no
  warnings and every Test-verified requirement has a procedure.
- **Remaining deficiencies, limitations, constraints.** No solver behavior is
  verified yet (30 requirements at `surface`, 69 at `pending`). Procedures TST-004,
  TST-006 through TST-009, and TST-018 through TST-030 are specified but not yet
  written. Code coverage is not measured (SWE-189, planned for phase 8).
- **Impact of the test environment.** None; the suite has no external dependencies and
  ran in 33 ms after a 17 s compile.
